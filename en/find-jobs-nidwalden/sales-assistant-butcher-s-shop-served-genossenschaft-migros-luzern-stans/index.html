Help shape the shopping experience at Migros! As a salesperson at the butcher shop, you present and sell our high-quality meat products. With your specialist knowledge and passion, you will advise our customers and make their shopping an enjoyable experience! Mansioni

  • Quality, data and freshness control

  • Reliable and precise way of working
