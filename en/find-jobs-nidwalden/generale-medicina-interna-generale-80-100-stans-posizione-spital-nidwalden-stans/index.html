Full description

The Nidwalden Hospital is a modern acute care hospital with 90 beds, around 600 dedicated employees and is responsible for the extended basic care. As a LUKS Group company, we are part of a powerful network that is one of the leading hospital groups in Switzerland. Together, we provide around the clock with passion and commitment for coordinated, digitally connected and accessible healthcare for all.

We are looking for one or more by 1 January 2028 or by agreement Tasks: • Medical, organizational and economic overall responsibility for the Clinic of General Internal Medicine at the Stans site with independent location responsibility • Ensuring and continuously developing high-quality stationary internistic care as the main pillar of extended basic care in the canton of Nidwalden • Co-responsibility for interdisciplinary cooperation with the Emergency Center as well as for seamless and high-quality patient care along the entire treatment path • Strategic and conceptual development of the clinic based on the medical strategy of the LUKS Group, the cantonal service mandate and the development goals of the Nidwalden Hospital • Ensuring the highest quality standards in diagnostics and therapy • Assume the technical and personnel management of the medical team and are responsible for medical further education and the training of assistant doctors WB category B Requirements • Specialist title General Internal Medicine, additional qualification such as e.g. B Leadership, health management, health economics or education beneficial • Very broad and proven internalist expertise • Several years of leadership experience as a cadre doctor, networking in the region beneficial • Excellent leadership, social and communication skills as well as enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation • Sound knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system • Strategic, innovative thinking and interest in continuous clinical and organizational development • Interest and understanding of business relationships as well as openness with regard to digitalization • Ability to accompany change processes, integrative personality • High patient orientation and quality awareness We offer: • Management and further development of the clinic in a very well-positioned regional hospital with close links to the Lucerne Center Hospital • Exciting leadership at the Stans location – within a dynamic, university hospital group with a clear future strategy • Close interdisciplinary cooperation both in Stans and in the LUKS Group • Attractive employment conditions and an appreciative, collegial working environment
