An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence; at the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture.
