Responsible for Applications:r Railway Planning Systems / Product Owner 80-100% Stansstad, admission as of January 2027 or n.V.

You can do that with us.

  • You take responsibility for our Nexfeld railway planning system and develop it strategically and operationally.
  • You will analyze and prioritize requirements from the specialist areas and develop suitable solutions together with our partners.
  • You coordinate releases, updates and further developments and ensure stable and reliable system operation.
  • You are the central contact person between departments, IT and software suppliers and ensure smooth cooperation.
  • You will recognize optimization and automation potential and actively drive forward the digitalization of our railway production.
  • You accompany changes, support our users and ensure high system quality and user satisfaction.
  • You are in direct contact with our users and ensure a positive support experience. At the same time, you use the knowledge gained to actively shape the future of our product and to continuously increase its added value.

You bring this luggage with you

  • You have a higher specialist training or a university degree in business informatics, computer science, business administration or a comparable subject area.
  • You bring several years of professional experience as a product owner, application manager, business analyst or project manager.
  • You have experience with complex software solutions and have a good understanding of IT interfaces and agile working methods.
  • You think analytically and networked, work in a structured way and like to develop practical solutions.
  • You convince with communication strength, initiative and negotiating skills and understand how to bring different stakeholders together.
  • You communicate in German and have good knowledge of English. Knowledge of the railway industry or planning and scheduling systems is a plus.

Our extras for your trip

Do you want to make a difference and contribute your expertise and personality in an interesting environment? In addition to contemporary working conditions, short decision-making paths and good development opportunities, you will find a committed team and a culture characterized by appreciation, respect and personal responsibility.
