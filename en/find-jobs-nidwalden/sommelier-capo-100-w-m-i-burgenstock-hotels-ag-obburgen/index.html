Head Sommelier 100% (f/m/i) - Bürgenstock Resort --> An icon among Swiss hotels: The history of the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne began in 1873 - a story that continues to impress and touch today. High up on the Bürgenberg lies an oasis of relaxation and a place of first-class hospitality. The Bürgenstock Resort and its around 700 employees from 54 nations offer the perfect setting for dazzling moments that will be remembered. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two overnight accommodations The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Taverne 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Ten restaurants, bars & lounges are the showcase for culinary excellence

To strengthen our team, we are looking for one from now on in 2026 or by appointment: Head Sommelier 100% (f/m/i) With over 1,200 positions in the wine cellar, the Bürgenstock Resort is an address that delights wine lovers from all over the world. As Head Sommelier (100%), you will take responsibility for the design and strategic direction of our wine range, lead a motivated sommelier team and set new standards in wine and gastronomy culture. This is what awaits you - your stage for excellence Strategic responsibility: design and maintenance of the wine lists as well as strategic orientation of the wine range in coordination with the Director of F&B Operational perfection: wine purchasing for the entire resort, including selection of suppliers, price negotiations and contract processing Sales and advice: planning and advice on wine selection at events, active wine sales to guests as well as conducting wine tastings Team leadership: recruiting, leading and developing a motivated sommelier team as well as creating rosters Quality assurance: order and care of the wine cellar, daily wine distribution to the outlets as well as compliance with budget requirements in terms of sales and cost of goods Further education and training: Carrying out wine training courses for service employees and promoting wine knowledge throughout the entire team
