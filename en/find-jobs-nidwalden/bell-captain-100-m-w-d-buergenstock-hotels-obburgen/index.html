Full description

With more than 165 years of history, Hotel Schweizerhof Bern & Spa is the most traditional hotel in the city. As a member of the Bürgenstock Collection, our hotel impresses with excellent service and first-class services for guests from all over the world. Our corporate culture is shaped above all by our flat hierarchy, collegial cooperation and a decent dose of humor and passion. We are convinced that only satisfied employees can inspire our guests - that is why we offer a working environment that motivates, inspires and inspires best performance. We are proud to have officially received the award as "Great Place To Work® Switzerland". This certification is based on the ratings of our employees, who consider our workplace culture to be excellent. Hotel Schweizerhof Bern & Spa Great Place To Work® Switzerland. Are you ready to create outstanding guest experiences with us while learning and developing? Then you are exactly right with us! We look forward to your application for the open position as

Bell Captain 100% (m/f/d)

Your daily work

  • Leadership of our Bell team with 2 employees, including assignment planning and training
  • Warm welcome and farewell of our guests
  • Baggage service and storage
  • Acceptance and parking of our guests' vehicles (Valet Parking)
  • Post office and messengers in the city
  • Professional handling of guest concerns and complaints
  • Keep track of major events, group travel and lots of luggage
  • Access control at the hotel entrance

What we want from you

  • Professional experience in a comparable position in the upscale hospitality industry
  • First leadership experience or motivation to take responsibility for a small team
  • Very good knowledge of German and English
  • Every other foreign language is an advantage
  • Pronounced guest orientation and a sovereign appearance, even in hectic situations
  • Organizational talent and the overview if there is a lot going on in the lodge
  • A sense of responsibility and a forward-looking, independent working method
  • Willingness to work irregular hours incl. Weekend services

For this position, we can only consider applications with a valid driving license Cat. B.
