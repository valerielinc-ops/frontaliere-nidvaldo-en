Night Butler 100% (m/f/d)

Your daily work The position is composed of 60% Night Butler and 40% Night Auditor. Night Butler (60%)

  • First contact person for our guests during the night
  • Valet parking and luggage service for late arrivals and early departures
  • Cleaning and ordering work in the lobby and public areas
  • Completion of guest wishes and room service at night
  • Security tours in the house

Night audit (40%)

  • Implementation of the end of the day in the property management system
  • Control of invoices, payments and cash
  • Preparation of daily reports for the management and departments
  • Check-in and check-out during the night

What we want from you

  • First experience in the hotel industry, ideally at the reception, in the night audit or as a night butler
  • Number affinity and an exact, independent working method
  • Safe handling of the PC, knowledge of a property management system (e.g. Opera) is an advantage
  • Pleasure in practical work and willingness to take part in cleaning tasks
  • Fluent German and good English skills, every other foreign language is an advantage
  • High guest orientation, discretion and sense of responsibility
  • Readiness for regular night assignments as well as weekend and holiday services

For this position we can only consider applicants with a valid Swiss driving license (cat. B).
