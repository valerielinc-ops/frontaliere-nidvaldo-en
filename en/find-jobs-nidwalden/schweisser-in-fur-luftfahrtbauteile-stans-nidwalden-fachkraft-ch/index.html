Full description

The activity includes the production of components by means of TIG welding. The company operates strictly according to aviation standards and relies on high-quality materials.   • Safe handling of grinding tools
  • Routine for straightening work
  • Knowledge of German and English is an advantage
  • Attractive framework conditions
  • Good remuneration
  • Long-term employment
