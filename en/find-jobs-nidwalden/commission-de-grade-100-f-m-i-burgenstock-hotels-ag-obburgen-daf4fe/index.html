Full description

An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations provide excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence; at the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a “Great Place to Work” in Switzerland – an award that fills the entire team with pride. To strengthen our team, we will be looking for one from July 2026 or by agreement:

Commis de rank 100% (f/m/i)

As a Commis de Rang, with passion, professionalism and a keen sense of detail, you ensure that every visit becomes an unforgettable experience. This is what awaits you – Your stage for excellence

  • Responsibility for a smooth and efficient service flow in compliance with all internal quality standards
  • Competent and courteous care of our demanding guests
  • Ensuring first-class food and drink services
  • Creating a welcoming, pleasant atmosphere that offers our guests an all-round positive experience

Your profile – how you fit perfectly with us You have completed a service education or already gained experience in a similar position in the upscale gastronomy or hotel industry. You communicate safely and fluently in German and English, other foreign languages are a plus. A structured, careful way of working is just as much a strength as resilience and flexibility. When dealing with international guests, you act attentively, sensitively and service-oriented. You recognize needs, enjoy selling and always appear friendly and professional. If you actively contribute your knowledge, want to learn new things and have a passion for excellent service, you are a great fit for us.

In addition to a modern workplace, you benefit from numerous attractive employee offers and perks. The staff house has its own gym, a TV lounge, a pool room and a library. In addition, you will enjoy discounts in our restaurants as well as preferential conditions for you, your family and friends for overnight stays in our hotels. Other benefits include perks for tennis, golf, sporting goods, insurance, group courses and REKA checks. We also support your mobility: Public transport is financially supported and parking is available at attractive conditions. We invest in your future: With access to the Beekeeper employee app, a wide range of further education offers, internal development opportunities and first-class career prospects, we create the framework for your personal and professional development. She expects the chance to be part of a world-class resort, to actively contribute to the joint success and to continuously develop in an inspiring environment. We look forward to your complete application documents with letters of motivation, employment certificates and photo.
