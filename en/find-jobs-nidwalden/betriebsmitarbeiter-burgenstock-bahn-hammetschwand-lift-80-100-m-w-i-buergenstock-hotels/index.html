Full description

An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations provide excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence; at the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a “Great Place to Work” in Switzerland – an award that fills the entire team with pride. To strengthen our team, we will be looking for one from January 2027 or by agreement

Company employees Bürgenstock Bahn & Hammet wall lift 80-100% (m/w/i)

  • The main task is to operate and monitor the Bürgenstock railway and the Hammetschwand lift.
  • Guest contact and their support as well as tourist advice (ticketing, information, etc.)
  • Performing daily checks at the installation
  • Ensuring the smooth daily operation of the installation concerned
  • Implementation of minor maintenance work as well as assistance in the annual revision margins
  • Communicative and friendly treatment of guests
  • A well-groomed appearance, good manners and social competence
  • Independent and reliable working methods, flexibility and willingness to cooperate
  • Good handling of the common MS Office programs
  • Good ability to express and communicate both in German and English
  • Further foreign language skills are beneficial
  • Joy and motivation in learning new skills and abilities in the field of rail and lift operation incl. Salvage drills
  • Flexibility and readiness for evening and weekend services
  • Preferably, they have already gained experience as a machinist or technician in the cable car line (no mandatory requirement)

If you enjoy dealing with guests, are communicative and resilient and do not shy away from weekend commitments, you are at the right place with us. Take the unique opportunity of a varied task with the potential to learn in the most beautiful mountain environment.
