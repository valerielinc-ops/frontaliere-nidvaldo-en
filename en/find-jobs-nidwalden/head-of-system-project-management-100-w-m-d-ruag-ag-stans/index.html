Full description

Approximately 3,000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate make a significant contribution to the security of Switzerland every day. They ensure that the Swiss Armed Forces and other operational and security organizations can comprehensively fulfill their tasks at all times.

What you can achieve

  • Leading the System & Project Management team
  • Acting as the point of contact for customers and OEMs of the assigned systems/SLAs
  • Taking responsibility for and coordinating all system and project activities of the assigned systems and SLAs in Drones & Products
  • Directing, controlling, and steering all projects within the assigned portfolio
  • Ensuring compliance with all contractual obligations and representing the company to all relevant stakeholders and customers
  • Taking over own systems and projects as needed to relieve resource planning and ensure backup coverage

What you bring

  • Technical or commercial education with further training in project management is an advantage
  • Several years of professional experience in project management and leadership experience
  • Experience in system management and customer service
  • Very good German and English skills in writing and speaking
  • Customer-oriented, highly communicative, and assertive personality
  • Structured and solution-oriented working style

Do you not meet all the requirements one hundred percent? Apply anyway. We are always looking for talented and motivated individuals who want to enrich our team. We place great value on diversity and equal opportunity, and our goal is to build a diverse team where everyone can fully develop their personal strengths. In particular, we encourage all individuals, regardless of their gender, who may have concerns about accepting a position in an environment close to the military, to apply.

About the department

System Management is responsible for the lifecycle management of the supported platforms in close collaboration with Engineering, Project Management, Operations, and Sales. This includes various air and support systems such as runway systems, aircraft, simulators, guided weapons maintenance, as well as EKMS/SKL.
