Full description

An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations ensure excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence; at the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a "Great Place to Work" in Switzerland - an award that fills the entire team with pride.

To strengthen our team, we are looking for an agreement

Lingerie employees 100% (f/m/i)

Their tasks include

  • management of incoming and outgoing linen and uniform (sorting, washing and ironing)
  • Operation, loading and maintenance of washing machines
  • creating the Mise en Place of cleaning utensils for the various outlets
  • Ironing service, sewing, cleaning of guest linen and cleaning in the lingerie (sucking, dusting, wet wiping floor)
  • sorting the dirty linen
  • Loading the Conveyer
  • Assistance in uniform management, inventory and on the floor
  • the transport of laundry in the resort with a transporter

Preferably, you bring experience from similar positions in the 5* hotel industry or with you. We presuppose reliability, resilience and teamwork. They work precisely and well structured and act calmly and efficiently even in hectic phases. You have a valid driving licence of category B (mandatory for this position). We expect a well-groomed, friendly and courteous attitude. Foreign languages are an additional advantage in dealing with our employees and guests.
