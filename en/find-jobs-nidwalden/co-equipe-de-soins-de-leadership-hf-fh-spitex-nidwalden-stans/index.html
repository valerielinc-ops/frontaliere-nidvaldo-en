Full description

160 employees. Hundreds of clients. Every day on the road in outpatient care. Good care is not only created at home by our clients. It is also created where teams are led, decisions are made and new ideas are implemented. This is exactly where you come into play. You want to strengthen people, take responsibility and actively shape changes – without losing your connection to the nursing practice? You value the exchange at eye level and are convinced that good leadership is created together? Then we should get to know each other.

And you in the middle of it: As co-team leader, you take responsibility for your team, actively shape developments and, together with your co-leader, ensure a strong daily care.

Co-Team Management Care (HF/FH) / from 60% PEN What you really expect from us:

  • You lead a dedicated care team in the co-leadership model, which distributes responsibility on several shoulders.
  • They promote an esteemed and learning-oriented team culture so that employees can develop their potential.
  • They accompany development and change processes and actively contribute their own ideas.
  • They ensure quality care and care at home and help people live at home for a long time.
  • They work closely with clients, relatives, doctors and other network partners.
  • You stay close to the practice and understand the challenges and needs of your team first hand.

Why it's worth it for you

  • Co-Leadership model: sharing responsibility, making decisions together and benefiting from each other.
  • Design room: Your ideas are welcome and can influence the future of our organization.
  • Closeness to the practice: They do not lead from a distance, but stay on the pulse of everyday care.
  • Further development: Technically and personally grow – with the support of your superiors.
  • Short distances: decisions are made where knowledge is available.
  • A meaningful task: You directly influence the quality of life of our clients and accompany your team through everyday life.
  • Flexible working conditions: We are looking for solutions that fit your life situation.

What we count on with you

  • Your training: Diploma as a nursing specialist HF/FH.
  • Your experience: outpatient care - ideally you already know the Spitex area.
  • Your leadership potential: You bring leadership experience or want to take the next step into a leadership role.
  • Your working methods: Organized, independent and with joy of responsibility.
  • Your communication: You create trust, promote cooperation and meet people on an equal footing.
  • Your mobility: category B driver's pass and your own vehicle.
