Full description

The activity includes the production of components by means of TIG welding. The company operates strictly according to aviation standards and relies on high-quality materials. You benefit from attractive conditions and a secure workplace. On behalf of our customer we are looking for one or more activities

  • manufacturing welding components for aviation
  • carrying out welding work exclusively in the TIG process
  • Working in aircraft construction according to DIN 24393
  • Processing materials of groups A, B and D
  • Covering tests per material group in the required scope
  • Requirements
  • Several years of practice in TIG welding of steel, stainless steel or aluminium
  • Experience with personnel certifications such as welder tests according to ISO 9606
  • Safe handling of grinding tools
  • Routine for straightening work
  • Knowledge of German and English is an advantage
  • Attractive framework conditions
  • Good remuneration
  • Long-term employment
