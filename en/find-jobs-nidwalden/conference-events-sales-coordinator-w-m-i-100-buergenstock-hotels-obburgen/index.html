Full description

An icon of the Swiss hotel industry and a legend since its sensational opening in 1873. Located on the Bürgenberg, a 500-meter-high rocky outcrop in central Switzerland, the Bürgenstock Resort Lake Lucerne stands out for its bold design, groundbreaking sustainability, breathtaking landscapes and state-of-the-art leisure activities. Around 700 employees from 54 nations provide excellent hospitality and provide the perfect setting for moments beyond imagination. Three exclusive hotels, the Bürgenstock Hotel & Alpine Spa (5Superior) with the two accommodation options The Contemporary and The Heritage, the Waldhotel by Bürgenstock (5 Superior) and the Tavern 1879 (3*) are available to guests. Each of these houses knows how to create a unique atmosphere. Twelve restaurants, lounges and bars are the showcase for culinary excellence; at the award-winning Bürgenstock Alpine Spa and Waldhotel Spa, guests will find the ultimate relaxation. The resort is not only a dream destination for travelers from all over the world, but also a workplace with an excellent corporate culture. For the third consecutive year, Bürgenstock Resort has been officially certified as a “Great Place to Work” in Switzerland – an award that fills the entire team with pride. To strengthen our team, we will be looking for one from January 2027 or by agreement:

Conference & Events Sales Coordinator (f/m/i) 100%

Its extensive main tasks include

  • Ensure that all administrative tasks are completed successfully, correctly and on time, and provide up-to-date information as needed
  • Ensure excellent customer service is provided before, during and after events
  • Advise clients and create customized offers and event concepts, including tastings, venue recommendations and site visits
  • Processing customer inquiries, creating offers and negotiating contracts to optimize business results
  • Assume responsibility for an assigned customer portfolio and ensure long-term customer relationships and follow-up orders
  • Work closely with the proactive MICE sales team to increase lead conversion and overall sales

They have completed vocational training and experience in the upscale and international hotel industry and have ideally already gained F&B knowledge. We require very good know-how with MS office and hotel reservation programs, communication and negotiation skills as well as solution-oriented behavior, an independent working method and detail accuracy. Responsibility and organizational awareness as well as a safe, friendly attitude, pleasure in dealing with people and very good language skills in German and English distinguish you. If you have already worked with OPERA, this is a big plus. They think and act networked, are customer-oriented and recognize priorities by themselves.
